## GPA Calculator
This uses a 5.0 grade scale where A is 5 points, B is 4 points, C is 3 points, D is 2 points, E is one point, and F is zero points.

### Logic
1. Users are asked to input their name and the number of courses offered.
2. They are then prompted to enter the course name, the unit of the course, and the grade they got for the course.
3. After users have inputted the details, GPA is calculated thus;

$$
GPA = \frac{\sum_{}^{}Grade \hspace{0.3em} Score \times Unit}{No \hspace{0.3em} of \hspace{0.3em} Courses}
$$

5. After calculation, the result is displayed.

## Palindrome Checker
This checks if a word, phrase or number is a palindrome (same backward and forward pattern). Regular expression is used to get all the words before they are joined to each other. All words are made lowercase (to make the checker case-insensitive). The combined words are then reversed and checked against the actual combined words.

## Word Frequency Counter
All words are taken from the input using regular expressions and are made lowercase. We then map each word to 1 and increment it when it occurs again.
